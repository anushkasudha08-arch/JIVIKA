Private by design

What this website does with your information

  • Health entries stay in this browser. There is no server that receives them.
  • Every health article opens without an account, a phone number or an email.
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels and no selling of personal information.
  • No diagnosis and no medicine names — only education, and a push to a real doctor.

On a shared phone

Practical steps that take under a minute

Lock the phone first

Your screen lock is the real password. Anyone who can unlock the phone can open this browser.

Use a private window

In private or incognito mode everything is forgotten when you close it. Export a copy first if you want to keep records.

Delete before handing it over

One button in “What is stored on this device” removes health records without touching anything else.

Watch out for fraud

JIVIKA never calls or messages to ask for an Aadhaar number, an OTP, money or medicine orders. That kind of call is a scam — report it on 181.
