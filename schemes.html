Your application checklist

Most applications fail because a document is missing or the form is never followed up. Tick these off as you go — your progress stays on this device.

Keep these ready

Copies, not originals, where possible

  • Aadhaar card and ration card
  • Bank passbook in your own name, linked to Aadhaar
  • MCP card (mother and child protection card) if pregnant
  • Any previous prescriptions, reports or discharge papers
  • Two passport-size photographs, for some schemes

Who can help you apply

All of these are free

  • ASHA worker — pregnancy registration and maternity schemes.
  • Anganwadi worker — nutrition, maternity benefit and the MCP card.
  • Common Service Centre (CSC) — online forms for a small official fee.
  • Hospital help desk — Ayushman and free-treatment counters.

Stay alert

Protect yourself from scheme fraud

Women are often targeted by people who charge for free forms, or ask for documents “for processing”.

Warning signs

Walk away and tell someone

  • Someone asks for cash, a “file charge” or a commission to apply.
  • A person offers to keep your original Aadhaar or ration card.
  • You are asked for an OTP, ATM PIN or bank password.
  • The website does not end in .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • Someone promises money “today” without any form or receipt.

Call 181 (women's helpline) or tell your ASHA worker.

Safe habits

Small steps that protect you

  • Apply in person at a government office, or on the official website only.
  • Keep a photo of every form and receipt on your phone.
  • Write down the application number and the date you applied.
  • Ask for the name and designation of the person who helped you.
  • Use the free 104 helpline if you are unsure whether a scheme is real.

Not finding your scheme? Many state and district programmes are not listed here. Ask at the district health office, the zilla panchayat, or your local MLA/ward office — and check the state health department website.
