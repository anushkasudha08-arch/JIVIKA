Safety first

How the assistant decides what to say

Every answer is sorted into one of three levels. This is what decides whether you get home care advice or an emergency warning.

Emergency

Call 108 now

Words like chest pain, cannot breathe, fainting, heavy bleeding or thoughts of self-harm skip all other advice. The assistant tells you to call 108 or 14416 and to tell someone near you.

See a doctor

Within a day or two

Fever that will not settle, burning urine, unusual discharge, a new lump, bleeding after menopause, or pain that is getting worse. The assistant explains what to say and where to go.

Home care

Watch for warning signs

Mild cramps, a light cold, tiredness after a busy week. The assistant gives safe home suggestions and tells you which warning signs mean the problem is no longer mild.

The assistant never prescribes medicines, never changes a prescribed dose and never says “you have” a disease. If an answer does not match your situation, ignore it and speak to a qualified doctor, nurse or ASHA worker.
